# Historical field event expansion

## Player-facing changes

The three historical event locations now require sustained play in the existing walkable maps. Each has 18 moving residents across three connected areas, nine distinct objectives, at least six decisions that require entering a particular person's body, and a multi-person scene in each area. Players must hear twelve residents, complete seven objectives, visit all three areas, and complete six role-specific actions before choosing the event's closing action.

The new group scenes bring named participants to the object the player used and show short, event-specific exchanges after the choice. The later group encounter can expose a different choice based on an earlier action. Each decision remains in the historical event record; later character dialogue receives up to six of the player's most recent authored decisions for that event.

## Event content boundaries

The added personal goals and conversations concern fictional residents in the scene. They do not alter the documented outcome of the historical event. The historical event catalog remains the authority for dates, fixed outcomes, source links, and source ranges. No new historical claim was added in the field scenes.

## Design references

- Obsidian's description of *Pentiment* highlights consequences that follow characters and affect a tightly connected community over generations. This expansion adapts the community response and persistent choice record, while keeping each historical outcome fixed. [Obsidian, Pentiment announcement](https://www.obsidian.net/news/obsidian/obsidian-entertainment-announces-pentiment)
- The official *Pentiment* site describes consequences playing out over generations. The field scenes carry authored choices into later character dialogue, while stopping short of changing the source-backed historical result. [Pentiment official site](https://pentiment.obsidian.net/)
- The *Suzerain* official site presents decisions as shaping people and institutions, and its announcement material emphasizes implementing decisions through systems. The added field choices therefore change later available options and how residents gather, rather than ending at a selected dialogue label. [Suzerain official site](https://www.suzeraingame.com/)
- A 2024 Games and Narrative Review paper on *Pentiment* discusses narrative agency through tangible consequences and the player's allocation of time. This informed the requirements to travel through all three areas, switch roles, and spend time hearing several residents before the closing choice. [Paradoxically Meaningful Choices, GDC/Games and Narrative Review](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2025/GNR/Papers/CrisseyNoah_DIGIPEN_GNR2024.pdf)
