# V37 economy pilot status

## Current baseline

The existing V36 simulation has 36 fictional city residents with money, carried items, relationships, needs, and scheduled work. Its 72 scheduled work actions did not settle wages. Only two schedules used physical exchange tasks, and resident records had no recurring rent or debt contracts.

## Implemented vertical slice

The Mikhail/Roman unloading shift and three printshop tasks now produce wage claims: Sofia pays Ekaterina for proof work, and Ekaterina pays Stepan and Nikita for typesetting work. A worker with an unpaid claim chooses a deterministic `COLLECT_WAGE` goal and routes to the named payer. Money transfers only when both residents are alive, not possessed, in the same map and adjacent, and the payer can cover the amount. A claim stays due if any condition fails; the simulation creates no replacement money.

The baker's existing bread-for-cash exchange transfers bread into a separate market-stock ledger, so the seller's personal meal cannot consume goods held for sale. The seller offers the bread only on the market map during authored opening windows. When hungry and able to afford food, an unpossessed resident with access to the market chooses the stocked seller, walks to her current location, pays from their own wallet, and eats the bread. The seller receives the money, stock is removed, hunger falls, and a nearby player gets a witnessed journal entry. The 1-kopeck base price and hunger relief are explicitly fictional gameplay balances, not historical estimates.
